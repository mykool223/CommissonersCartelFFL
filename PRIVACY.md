# Privacy Policy — Commissioners Cartel

*Last updated: 21 August 2026*

This is a private app for a single twelve-person fantasy football league. It is
not a commercial product, is not distributed publicly, and exists only so the
league can read its own scores, news and messages in one place.

## What the app stores

**Your email address.** Used to sign you in and to check you are on the league
roster. Nothing else. There is no password, because the app never asks for one.

**A display name.** Taken from the league roster, or from the part of your email
before the `@` if the commissioner did not set one.

**Which fantasy team is yours.** Only if you choose it in Settings. This is an
identifier from ESPN, not personal information.

**What you write.** Messages you post in the league thread, and which option you
picked in a poll.

That is the complete list.

## What the app does not do

- **No tracking.** No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no third-party
  analytics SDKs, no crash reporting services.
- **No selling or sharing.** Your data is not sold, rented, shared with
  advertisers, or used to train anything.
- **No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.** The app never asks
  for these permissions because it never uses them.
- **No background collection.** The app only does anything while you have it
  open.

## Where it is stored

League data is held in a [Supabase](https://supabase.com) database controlled by
the league commissioner. Access is restricted at the database level: you can
only read the league's content if your email is on the roster, and you can only
ever see your own poll vote.

Sign-in emails are delivered by [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com), which sees your
email address in order to send you a link.

Scores, schedules and team names come from ESPN and are read live rather than
copied. Player news headlines come from The Fantasy Footballers.

## Who can see what

- Other members of your league can see your display name, your team, your
  messages and the total votes on a poll.
- **Nobody, including the commissioner, can see which option you personally
  voted for.** That is enforced by the database, not by the app.
- Nobody outside the league can read the thread, the member list, or poll votes.

## Keeping or deleting your data

Your sign-in session is stored in the iOS Keychain on your own device and is
removed when you sign out.

To have your account and everything attached to it deleted, ask the league
commissioner or email **commissionerscartel@outlook.com**. There is no
retention period — it is removed when you ask.

## Children

The app is for members of a private adult fantasy football league and is not
directed at children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be at this address, and the
